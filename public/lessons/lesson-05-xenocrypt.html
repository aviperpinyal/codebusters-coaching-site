Lesson 5

XENOCRYPT

Aristocrat in Spanish — foreign letter frequencies

What is a Xenocrypt?

  • A monoalphabetic substitution cipher — just like Aristocrat
  • Key difference: the plaintext is in Spanish (in Science Olympiad)
  • Word spaces are preserved (like Aristocrat, not Patristocrat)
  • Appears as DC at all levels — can be Decrypt or Cryptanalysis
  • The test will indicate it's a Xenocrypt

Why Spanish Matters

  • Spanish has different letter frequencies than English
  • Spanish top letters: E A O S R N I D L C T U M P
  • Most common word: DE, LA, EL, EN, LOS
  • Common endings: -CIÓN, -MENTE, -ANDO, -IENDO
  • Letters Q, K, W, X are very rare in Spanish
If the test says Xenocrypt — switch to Spanish frequency tables immediately.

Useful Spanish Words to Know

  • DE — of / from
  • LA / LAS — the (fem.)
  • EL / LOS — the (masc.)
  • EN — in
  • QUE — that / which
  • UNA / UN — a / an
  • ES — is
  • SE — reflexive particle
  • CON — with
  • POR — for / by
  • NO — no / not
  • SU — his / her / their

Worked Example

Ciphertext
QT WMBQF MTH JMHQF XW QHDMSQWBAR
  • 2-letter word QT — try EL, LA, DE, EN
  • Repeated Q in QT and WMBQF and JMHQF → Q is probably E
  • With Q=E: QT = E? → try EL, ES, EN
  • T=L → check XW → fill in and iterate

Strategy Summary

  • Confirm it's Spanish (test label says Xenocrypt)
  • Count cipher letter frequencies → map to E A O S R N …
  • Find 1-letter words: only A or Y in Spanish
  • Find 2-letter words: DE, LA, EL, EN, ES, UN, NO
  • Use common endings: -CIÓN, -ANDO, -MENTE
The test always provides a Spanish frequency guide — use it!

Key Takeaways

  • Xenocrypt = Aristocrat with Spanish plaintext
  • Use Spanish letter frequencies (E A O S R N …), not English
  • Learn 10–15 common Spanish words for pattern matching
  • Appears as DC at all levels

Exam Practice — Xenocrypt (C)

C-type: no key. Use Spanish letter frequencies: E, A, O, S, N, R, I, L, D, T are most common.
Crib: ES = AQ  (Spanish for "is")
Ciphertext
AH RKJKREIEAJSK AQ LKNAP
  • Fill E=A, S=Q everywhere first
  • Common 2-letter Spanish words: EL, LA, DE, EN, UN, ES, SE, AL
  • Answer is a well-known Spanish phrase

Answer — Xenocrypt (C)

Ciphertext
AH RKJKREIEAJSK AQ LKNAP
Plaintext (Spanish)
EL CONOCIMIENTO ES PODER
Translation
Knowledge is power
Crack path: AQ=ES gives E=A, S=Q → AH=EL → H=L → PKFKPAEAAJQK → CONOCIMIENTO (use Spanish word patterns)
