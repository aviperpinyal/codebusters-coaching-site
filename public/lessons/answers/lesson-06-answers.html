Lesson 6 — Answer Key

FRACTIONATED MORSE

Coach use only — do not distribute to students

Exam Q1 — FM (D) · G C K K E → COME

Step 1: cipher → trigraphs (STUDY key)
G→−·−   C→·X−   K→−−X   K→−−X   E→·XX
Step 2: concatenate
−·−·X−−−X−−X·XX
Step 3: split on X → Morse → letters
−·−· = C   −−− = O   −− = M   · = E
Answer: COME

Exam Q2 — FM (C) · H D N G U T O D F → CODE WIN

Crib CODE=HDNG reveals 4 key mappings
CODE Morse: −·−·X−−−X−··X· → trigraphs: −·− / ·X− / −−X / −··
∴ H→−·−   D→·X−   N→−−X   G→−··
Keyword: MAPLE (confirms remaining: U→X·X, T→X·−, O→−X·, F→·XX)
Full decode → Morse stream
−·−·X−−−X−··X·XX·−−X··X−·XX
Split on X → letters
−·−·=C −−−=O −··=D ·=E  |  ·−−=W ··=I −·=N
Answer: CODE WIN

HW 1 — FM (D) · Y O R → WIN

Step 1: trigraphs (STUDY key)
Y→·−−   O→X··   R→X−·
Step 2: concatenate
·−−X··X−·
Step 3: split on X → Morse → letters
·−− = W   ·· = I   −· = N
Answer: WIN

HW 2 — FM (D) · I V L L F → GOAL

Step 1: trigraphs
I→−−·   V→X−−   L→−X·   L→−X·   F→−··
Step 2: concatenate
−−·X−−−X·−X·−··
Step 3: split on X → letters
−−· = G   −−− = O   ·− = A   ·−·· = L
Answer: GOAL

HW 3 — FM (D) · F B O C → BEST

Step 1: trigraphs
F→−··   B→·X·   O→X··   C→·X−
Step 2: concatenate
−···X·X···X−
Step 3: split on X → letters
−··· = B   · = E   ··· = S   − = T
Answer: BEST
Teaching note: B's Morse is −··· (4 symbols) so the first trigraph is −·· (just the first 3), and the 4th dot carries into the next trigraph ·X·. This "fractionation" is the key concept — letters don't align with trigraphs.

HW 4 — FM (D) · K J O A E → MOVE

Step 1: trigraphs
K→−−X   J→−−−   O→X··   A→·−X   E→·XX
Step 2: concatenate
−−X−−−X···−X·XX
Step 3: split on X → letters
−− = M   −−− = O   ···− = V   · = E
Answer: MOVE
Teaching note: E→·XX at the end. The XX here is padding (end of stream) — in decoding, once we've identified M, O, V, E from the Morse segments, any trailing X's are ignored.
