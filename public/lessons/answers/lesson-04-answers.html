Exam Q1 — Baconian (C) · Problem

8 A/B groups of 5 → 2 words
AABBA ABBAB ABBAB AAABB   ABABA BABAA AAABA ABAAB

Exam Q1 — Solution: GOOD LUCK

Group-by-group decode
AABBA=G   ABBAB=O   ABBAB=O   AAABB=D  |  ABABA=L   BABAA=U   AAABA=C   ABAAB=K
Answer
GOOD LUCK
Teaching note: U and V share BABAA — context confirms U here. Same ambiguity on the test; U/V is always context-resolvable in competition problems.

Exam Q2 — Word Baconian (C) · Problem

UPPER=B, lower=A · 5 words per letter
20-word ciphertext → 4-letter answer
a good cipher HIDES its   meaning DEEP WITHIN the TEXT   so only those WHO KNOW   can read SECRET words within

Exam Q2 — Solution: CODE

Pattern extraction
Group 1: a(A) good(A) cipher(A) HIDES(B) its(A) → AAABA = C
Group 2: meaning(A) DEEP(B) WITHIN(B) the(A) TEXT(B) → ABBAB = O
Group 3: so(A) only(A) those(A) WHO(B) KNOW(B) → AAABB = D
Group 4: can(A) read(A) SECRET(B) words(A) within(A) → AABAA = E
Answer
CODE
