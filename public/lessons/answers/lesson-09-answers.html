Exam Q1 — Columnar (D) · Problem

Keyword: ACE  ·  Ranks: A=1, C=2, E=3  ·  Cipher: CEU OSL DRE
Segments (by rank): CEU (rank 1) · OSL (rank 2) · DRE (rank 3)
3 segments → 3 columns; 3 rows. Place by rank, read across rows.

Exam Q1 — Solution: CODES RULE

Grid (key order A C E, ranks 1 2 3)
A   C   E
1   2   3
C   O   D
E   S   R
U   L   E
Plaintext: CODES RULE  ·  Col A=CEU, col C=OSL, col E=DRE → read rows left to right ✓
