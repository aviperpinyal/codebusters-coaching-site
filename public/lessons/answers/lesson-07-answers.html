Exam Q1 — SEND + MORE = MONEY · Problem

S E N D
+ M O R E
M O N E Y
M=1 (carry), O=0 (follows from 1+S+carry = S in thousands col), then work right-to-left.

Solution

HW 3 — ACE + ACE = TEAM · Problem

A C E
+ A C E
T E A M
A appears in both the addend (hundreds) and the result (tens). E appears in both units of addend and hundreds of result.

HW 3 — Solution: TEAM = 1264

Solution
A=6   C=3   E=2   T=1   M=4
6 3 2
+ 6 3 2
1 2 6 4
TEAM = 1264  ·  Verify: 632 + 632 = 1264 ✓
Solve path: T=1. Units: 2E=?M (M is last digit). Tens: 2C+carry → tens of result = A. Hundreds: 2A+carry → hundreds of result = E. This cross-constraint (A in hundreds-of-addend matches A in tens-of-result, E in units-of-addend matches E in hundreds-of-result) is what makes it tricky.

HW 4 — ADD + ADD = BEE · Problem

A D D
+ A D D
B E E
BEE: EE means last two digits are identical. 2×D ends in E AND 2×D+carry also ends in E → carry = 0. So 2×D = ?E with no carry: 2D mod 10 = E, floor(2D/10) = 0 → D ≤ 4.

HW 4 — Solution: BEE = 822

Solution
A=4   D=1   B=8   E=2
4 1 1
+ 4 1 1
8 2 2
BEE = 822  ·  Verify: 411 + 411 = 822 ✓
Solve path: For EE in result with no carry between D positions: 2×D mod 10 = E and carry = 0 → D ≤ 4. Try D=1, E=2: works. Then 2×A+0 = B×10+E → A=4: 2×4=8, B=8 ✓. Other solution: D=4, E=8, A=? → 2×A=B×10+8 → 2A ends in 8 → A=4: 2×4=08 (B=0, but then B=0 and must check no conflicts): ADD=444 but D=A=4, conflict. So D=1 is the unique solution.
